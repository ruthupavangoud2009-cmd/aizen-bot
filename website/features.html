1. Sovereign Anti-Nuke Engine

KYOKA's Anti-Nuke operates at the gateway level. If an unauthorized administrator or compromised bot account attempts to delete channels, prune members, modify guild vanity URLs, or grant dangerous permissions, KYOKA triggers instantaneous role revocation and automatic lockdown before damage occurs.

  • ✓ Vanity URL Protection — Reverts vanity URL tampering automatically.
  • ✓ Role & Channel Shields — Blocks unauthorized mass creation, deletion, or permission overwrites.
  • ✓ Extra Owner Tier — Designate trusted co-owners who can bypass triggers without giving them full Discord guild ownership.
